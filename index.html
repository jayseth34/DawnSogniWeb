How to order

  1. Pick a design

    Tap any tee to see it up close. Tap the photo to zoom into the print.

  2. Choose fit and size

    Regular or oversized, S to XXL.

  3. Add to bag, send on WhatsApp

    Add one or more tees, then send the whole order in one message. We confirm and print it for you.
